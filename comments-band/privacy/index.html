Privacy Policy

Comments Band Draft Uploader

Who This Applies To

This privacy policy covers Comments Band Draft Uploader, a private tool used only by Bryan Weaver to upload videos to the @thecommentsband TikTok account. This is not a public service — there is no third-party sign-up or user registration.

What We Store

  • TikTok OAuth tokens — Used to authenticate with TikTok's API and upload drafts to the Creator Inbox. Scopes: user.info.basic, video.upload, video.list.
  • Video files — Temporarily processed to upload to TikTok's inbox as drafts. We do not use video.publish or Direct Post — all publishing is done manually in the TikTok app.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell or share data with third parties.
  • We do not display ads.
  • We do not collect emails or personal information from the public.
  • We do not auto-publish content to TikTok.

Data Deletion

To request deletion of stored OAuth tokens or any associated data, email weaverb9@gmail.com.
