$ Privacy Policy

The Comments Band uploader app · Effective October 19, 2026

This policy covers the uploader app that The Comments Band (Bryan Weaver) uses to publish videos to its own YouTube channel, @thecommentsband. The app is used only by Bryan Weaver to manage that one channel.

What the app accesses

The app uses Google and YouTube API access only to:

  • upload and manage videos on The Comments Band's own YouTube channel; and
  • read that channel's analytics.

It does not access any other YouTube channel or Google account.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal data from viewers or any other users.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share data with anyone.

How tokens are stored

The OAuth tokens the app receives from Google are stored privately and are used only to perform the actions above on The Comments Band's channel.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
